# 3D model credits and licences

Every model in this folder was generated by the Blender scripts in `tools/blender/`.
Some are built from parts of the public-domain packs below; the rest are procedural
geometry written for this project. No original Seven Kingdoms II art is included, and
none was traced. The original sprites were used only as on-screen references for
silhouettes and colours.

All third-party sources are **CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain): free for
commercial use, no attribution required. They are credited anyway.

| Pack | Author | Source | Licence | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| KayKit Character Pack: Adventurers 1.0 | Kay Lousberg (kaylousberg.com) | github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Character-Pack-Adventures-1.0 | CC0 | humanoid rig; body, head, arm, leg and cape meshes; swords, axes, crossbow, staff, knife, shields |
| KayKit Character Pack: Skeletons 1.0 | Kay Lousberg | github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Character-Pack-Skeletons-1.0 | CC0 | animation library (95 clips, `anims/humanoid.glb`); bodies of Kharshuf, Bregma and Exovum |
| LowPoly Animated Farm Animal Pack | Quaternius | opengameart.org/content/lowpoly-animated-farm-animal-pack | CC0 | horse rig and clips (horses, Chi Lin); cow mesh reshaped into elephants and the rhinoceros |
| Animated Animals Vol. 2 | Quaternius | opengameart.org/content/animated-animales-low-poly | CC0 | cat rig and clips (Jaguara) |
| Textured Cute Monster Pack | Quaternius | opengameart.org/content/textured-cute-monster-pack | CC0 | bee (Bheema) and crab rig (Tarmes) |

Made here (procedural, part of this GPL project): race headgear, procedural
weapons and shields, Fryhtan horns, tails, plates and growths, Greater Being wings and
halos, riding clips (`Ride_*`, derived from KayKit clips), jaguar pounce and death
clips, all buildings, town houses, lairs, siege weapons, carts, projectiles, trees,
bushes, rocks, mountains and deposits.

The packs are downloaded by `python tools/blender/sources.py fetch`. The script checks
that each OpenGameArt page still lists CC0. The packs are not stored in this
repository.
